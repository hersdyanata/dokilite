# Doki Lite Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

Doki Lite is a desktop widget app for Windows. It runs on your PC. We (the developer, Hersdyanata) don't run any servers for Doki Lite, and we don't collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.

## What stays on your PC

- **Your settings**, including widget layout, colors, launcher items, and calendar addresses, are saved in a file on your PC (`%APPDATA%\doki-lite\widgets.json`). They're never sent to us.
- **System information** shown in widgets, such as CPU, memory, disk, battery, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, volume, and brightness, is read from Windows on your PC and isn't sent anywhere.
- **Now Playing** shows the song from the app that's playing it, using Windows media controls. The visualizer measures the sound on your PC and doesn't record or send audio.

## What Doki Lite sends over the internet

- **Weather:** the city or coordinates you choose are sent to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) to get the forecast. Searching for a city sends what you type to Open-Meteo's geocoding service.
- **Calendar:** if you add an iCal address, Doki Lite downloads that calendar directly from its source (for example Google Calendar).
- **Updates (GitHub version only):** Doki Lite asks GitHub whether a new version is available. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store.

These services have their own privacy policies. Doki Lite sends them only what's needed for the feature you use.

## Purchases

Doki Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles payment and licensing. We don't see your payment details.

## Children

Doki Lite doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
